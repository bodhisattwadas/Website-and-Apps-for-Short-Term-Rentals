Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How fast is the 2-way multi-calendar sync between Airbnb and VRBO? +
StayOps communicates directly via official partner APIs rather than relying solely on slow iCal feeds. When a guest books on Airbnb, the corresponding dates are blocked on VRBO, Booking.com, and your direct website in under 400 milliseconds, completely preventing double-bookings.
How do smart lock codes work if a guest changes their arrival time? +
Door codes are automatically created to match the last 4 digits of the guest's verified phone number. If you approve early check-in or late check-out through the unified inbox, StayOps automatically updates the lock's active validity window remotely in real time.
Do our housekeepers need to pay for an account or install heavy software? +
No. Cleaners receive an automated link (via SMS or WhatsApp) that opens their tailored mobile checklist directly in their browser. They can check off items, upload inspection photos of the completed turnover, and report maintenance issues without needing a paid seat.
Can I accept direct bookings without paying OTA commissions? +
Yes! StayOps includes a built-in direct booking engine connected to Stripe. You keep 100% of your rental revenue minus standard 2.9% credit card processing fees, bypassing 15% to 20% platform commissions.
